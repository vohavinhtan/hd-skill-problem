# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $p$ be a prime with
$$
p\equiv1\pmod3,
$$
and choose $z\in\mathbb F_p$ such that
$$
z^2+z+1=0.
$$
Let $t$ be transcendental over $\mathbb F_p$, put $K=\mathbb F_p(t)$, and let $L$ be the splitting field over $K$ of
$$
F(X)=X^{p^2}-2zX^p+z^2X-t.
$$

Determine $\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$ up to isomorphism. Give the answer as a presentation with generators $r,a,b$, where exponents involving $z$ are read modulo $p$.

## Domain Explanation

This problem involves splitting fields, finite constant-field extensions, and Galois automorphisms,
which are part of Abstract Algebra and Field theory.
The problem also involves finite-field Frobenius actions and congruences,
which are part of Number Theory and modular arithmetic and congruences.
However, those ingredients control the internal action on the roots, while the main task is to reconstruct the splitting field and its full Galois group.
