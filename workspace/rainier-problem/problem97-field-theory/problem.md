# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $p$ be an odd prime with $p\equiv1\pmod3$, and let $z\in\{1,\ldots,p-1\}$ satisfy
$$
z^2+z+1\equiv0\pmod p.
$$
Let $t$ be transcendental over $\mathbb F_p$, set $K=\mathbb F_p(t)$, and consider
$$
F(X)=X^{p^2}-(z+1)X^p+zX-(t^p-t)\in K[X].
$$
Let $L$ be the splitting field of $F$ over $K$.

Determine $\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$ up to isomorphism. Give the answer as a presentation with generators $r,s$, where the exponent $z$ is read modulo $p$.

## Domain Explanation

This problem involves splitting fields, extension degrees, and Galois automorphisms, which are part of Abstract Algebra and Field theory.
The problem also involves congruences in the coefficient field and finite cyclic groups, which are part of Number Theory and modular arithmetic and congruences.
However, the congruence only specifies the action inside the constant-field extension, while the main task is to reconstruct the splitting field and its Galois group.
