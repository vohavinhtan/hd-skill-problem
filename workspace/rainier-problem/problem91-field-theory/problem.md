# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $p\geq5$ be a prime, let $t$ be transcendental over $\mathbb F_p$, and put
$$
q=t(t-1).
$$
Let $L_p$ be the splitting field over $\mathbb F_p(t)$ of
$$
F_p(X)=q^pX^{p^2}-(q^{p+1}+1)X^p+q^pX.
$$

Determine the genus of the function field $L_p$.

## Domain Explanation

The additive polynomial conceals a factorization of its Frobenius operator into two semilinear first-order factors. Reconstructing the root space produces a Kummer function field followed by an Artin-Schreier extension. The genus then depends on the divisor of the Kummer generator and a local different calculation at the two poles of the Artin-Schreier datum.
