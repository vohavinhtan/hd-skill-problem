# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $K$ be an imaginary quadratic field with ring of integers $\mathcal O$ whose ideal class group is cyclic of order $5$, generated by a class $C$. Assume the following splitting data.

The rational primes $2,3,5,11$ split as
$$
2\mathcal O=\mathfrak p_2\overline{\mathfrak p}_2,\qquad
3\mathcal O=\mathfrak p_3\overline{\mathfrak p}_3,\qquad
5\mathcal O=\mathfrak p_5\overline{\mathfrak p}_5,\qquad
11\mathcal O=\mathfrak p_{11}\overline{\mathfrak p}_{11},
$$
with ideal classes
$$
[\mathfrak p_2]=C,\qquad [\mathfrak p_3]=2C,\qquad [\mathfrak p_5]=2C,\qquad [\mathfrak p_{11}]=0.
$$
Also assume there exist elements $\gamma_1,\gamma_2,\gamma_3,\gamma_4\in\mathcal O$ with principal ideal factorizations
$$
(\gamma_1)=\mathfrak p_2^2\,\overline{\mathfrak p}_3,
\qquad
(\gamma_2)=\overline{\mathfrak p}_2\,\mathfrak p_5^3,
$$
$$
(\gamma_3)=\mathfrak p_3\,\overline{\mathfrak p}_5\,\mathfrak p_{11},
\qquad
(\gamma_4)=\overline{\mathfrak p}_{11}^{\,2}.
$$

For every split rational prime $\ell\notin\{2,3,5,11\}$, choose one prime ideal $\mathfrak q_\ell$ over $\ell$ and put
$$
[\mathfrak q_\ell]=c_\ell C,
\qquad c_\ell\in\{0,1,2,3,4\}.
$$
Let $Q$ be a finite set of inert rational primes. Fix nonnegative integers
$$
a,b,c,d,
\qquad e_\ell\ (\ell\in P),
\qquad f_q\ (q\in Q),
$$
where $P$ is a finite set of split primes disjoint from $\{2,3,5,11\}$, and define
$$
n=2^a3^b5^c11^d
\left(\prod_{\ell\in P}\ell^{e_\ell}\right)
\left(\prod_{q\in Q}q^{2f_q}\right).
$$

Let $\mathcal I_n$ be the set of principal integral ideals $I\subseteq\mathcal O$ such that $N(I)=n$. For $I\in\mathcal I_n$ and $j=1,2,3,4$, define
$$
D_j(I)=\#\{t\in\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z:\ t\gamma_j\in I\}.
$$

Determine the multiset
$$
\mathcal M_n=\{(D_1(I),D_2(I),D_3(I),D_4(I)):\ I\in\mathcal I_n\},
$$
with multiplicity. Your answer must be an explicit exponent-parametrized formula. In particular, it must include the exact congruence condition on the local split-prime exponents that is equivalent to principality of $I$.

## Domain Explanation

This problem is classified under Number Theory / Algebraic number theory because the central task is to enumerate principal ideals of a prescribed norm in a quadratic order with nontrivial class group and to compute local ideal-divisibility data prime by prime. The arithmetic content is the class-group congruence and the split/inert prime factorization; the final multiset is only a compact way of recording those algebraic-number-theoretic computations.
