# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let
$$
R=\mathbb Z[\sqrt2,\sqrt3],
\qquad
\lambda=3+2\sqrt2,
\qquad
\mu=2+\sqrt3.
$$
For integers $r,s\geq1$, define
$$
I_{r,s}=(\lambda^r-1,\ \mu^s-1)\subset R
$$
and
$$
G_{r,s}=R/I_{r,s}.
$$
Let $\sigma$ be the automorphism of $R$ sending $\sqrt2$ to $-\sqrt2$ and fixing $\sqrt3$, and let $\tau$ be the automorphism sending $\sqrt3$ to $-\sqrt3$ and fixing $\sqrt2$. Since
$$
\sigma(\lambda^r-1)=-\lambda^{-r}(\lambda^r-1),
\qquad
\tau(\mu^s-1)=-\mu^{-s}(\mu^s-1),
$$
the automorphisms $\sigma$ and $\tau$ descend to commuting involutions of $G_{r,s}$.

Define the double anti-trace subgroup
$$
K_{r,s}
=\{\xi\in G_{r,s}:\xi+\sigma(\xi)=0,\ \xi+\tau(\xi)=0\}.
$$
Also define
$$
E_{r,s}=(1-\sigma)(1-\tau)G_{r,s}.
$$
The inclusion $E_{r,s}\subseteq K_{r,s}$ follows from $\sigma^2=\tau^2=1$ and $\sigma\tau=\tau\sigma$. Define the anti-trace defect group
$$
D_{r,s}=K_{r,s}/E_{r,s}.
$$

Determine the invariant factor decomposition of $D_{r,s}$ as a finite abelian group, in terms of $r$ and $s$.

Here the invariant factor decomposition means an expression
$$
\mathbb Z/d_1\mathbb Z\oplus\cdots\oplus\mathbb Z/d_k\mathbb Z
$$
with $1<d_1\mid d_2\mid\cdots\mid d_k$.

## Domain Explanation

The problem concerns quotients of the biquadratic order $\mathbb Z[\sqrt2,\sqrt3]$ by ideals generated by powers of two independent norm-one units. The decisive structure is how the two algebraic conjugations act on this quotient and how their anti-trace kernel differs from the image of the double coboundary operator. The final object is a finite abelian group in invariant factor form, so Smith normal form is a supporting language, but the primary mechanism is algebraic number theory in a unit-generated quotient of a number-field order.
