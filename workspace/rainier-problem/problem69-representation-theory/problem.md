# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Fix integers
$$
m\geq3,
\qquad
1\leq j<2m.
$$
Let
$$
c=(1\ 2\ \cdots\ 2m)\in S_{2m},
\qquad
\zeta=\exp\!\left(\frac{2\pi\mathrm{i}}{2m}\right).
$$
Let $R_m$ be the ribbon skew diagram whose row lengths from top to bottom are
$$
1,\quad m-1,\quad m,
$$
with the leftmost cell of each non-bottom row directly above the rightmost cell of the row below. Let $S^{R_m}$ be the corresponding complex skew Specht module.

For every positive integer $q$, define the Ramanujan sum
$$
c_q(j)=\sum_{\substack{1\leq a\leq q\\ \gcd(a,q)=1}}
\exp\!\left(\frac{2\pi\mathrm{i}aj}{q}\right).
$$
Let $\mu_{m,j}$ be the multiplicity of the eigenvalue $\zeta^j$ in the action of $c$ on $S^{R_m}$.

Determine $\mu_{m,j}$ explicitly as a function of $m$ and $j$.

## Domain Explanation

This problem is classified under Abstract Algebra and Representation theory because it asks for an eigenvalue multiplicity obtained by restricting a skew Specht module of the symmetric group to the cyclic subgroup generated by a long cycle. The derivation depends on the representation-theoretic structure of ribbon Specht characters and their interaction with cyclic character sums.
