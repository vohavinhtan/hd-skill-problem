# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $p>3$ be prime. Let $\mathfrak L$ be the free Lie algebra over $\mathbb C$ on generators
$$
x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_{3p},
$$
and let $L_{3p}\subseteq\mathfrak L$ be its multilinear component: the span of Lie monomials in which every generator occurs exactly once.

The symmetric group $S_{3p}$ acts on $L_{3p}$ by permuting the generators. Put
$$
c=(1\ 2\ \cdots\ 3p),
\qquad
\zeta=\exp\!\left(\frac{2\pi\mathrm{i}}{3p}\right),
$$
and let $c$ act diagonally on $\Lambda^3L_{3p}$.

For $0\leq r<3p$, let $\nu_r$ denote the multiplicity of the eigenvalue $\zeta^r$ in this action.

Determine $\nu_1-\nu_p$ explicitly as a function of $p$.

## Domain Explanation

This problem is classified under Abstract Algebra and Representation theory because it studies the cyclic spectrum of a long cycle on an exterior power of the classical multilinear free-Lie representation of the symmetric group. The requested multiplicity difference depends on the character values of the Lie representation, the behavior of exterior powers under group elements and their powers, and restriction to the cyclic subgroup generated by the long cycle.
