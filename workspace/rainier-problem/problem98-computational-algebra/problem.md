# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Work in
$$
R=\mathbb Q[x,y,z]
$$
with lexicographic order $x>y>z$. Let $I=(h_1,h_2,h_3)$, where
$$
h_1=
x^3y+x^3z+2x^3+3x^2y^3+5x^2y^2z+5x^2y^2+2x^2yz^2+5x^2yz+x^2
+3xy^5+7xy^4z+4xy^4+5xy^3z^2+8xy^3z+xy^2z^3+4xy^2z^2+xy^2z+3xy^2
+2xyz^3+2xyz^2+2xyz+xz^5+xz^4+xz^3+xz^2-2xz-2x
+y^7+3y^6z+y^6+3y^5z^2+3y^5z+y^4z^3+3y^4z^2+2y^4+y^3z^3+3y^3z^2+2y^3z+y^3
+3y^2z^4+4y^2z^2+y^2z+2y^2+yz^6+3yz^4+yz^3+3yz^2+2yz-2y
+z^6+z^4+2z^3+3z^2-2z-9,
$$
$$
h_2=
2x^3y+3x^3z+4x^3+6x^2y^3+12x^2y^2z+11x^2y^2+6x^2yz^2+11x^2yz+2x^2
+6xy^5+15xy^4z+10xy^4+12xy^3z^2+20xy^3z+3xy^2z^3+10xy^2z^2+3xy^2z+5xy^2
+6xyz^3+2xyz^2+4xyz+3xz^5+xz^4+3xz^3+xz^2-6xz-2x
+2y^7+6y^6z+3y^6+6y^5z^2+9y^5z+2y^4z^3+9y^4z^2+4y^4+3y^3z^3+6y^3z^2+4y^3z+3y^3
+6y^2z^4+11y^2z^2+3y^2z+3y^2+2yz^6+9yz^4+2yz^3+7yz^2+4yz-5y
+3z^6+2z^4+6z^3+6z^2-8z-18,
$$
and
$$
h_3=
3x^3y+5x^3z+7x^3+9x^2y^3+19x^2y^2z+19x^2y^2+10x^2yz^2+19x^2yz+3x^2
+9xy^5+23xy^4z+17xy^4+19xy^3z^2+34xy^3z+5xy^2z^3+17xy^2z^2+5xy^2z+8xy^2
+10xyz^3+4xyz^2+6xyz+5xz^5+2xz^4+5xz^3+2xz^2-10xz-4x
+3y^7+9y^6z+5y^6+9y^5z^2+15y^5z+3y^4z^3+15y^4z^2+6y^4+5y^3z^3+9y^3z^2+6y^3z+5y^3
+9y^2z^4+18y^2z^2+5y^2z+5y^2+3yz^6+15yz^4+3yz^3+11yz^2+7yz-7y
+5z^6+3z^4+10z^3+10z^2-13z-30.
$$
Determine the reduced Gröbner basis of $I$ for this order.

## Domain Explanation

This problem involves polynomial ideals, polynomial-unimodular generator changes, and Gröbner basis reduction, which are part of Abstract Algebra and Computational algebra. The problem also involves nonlinear coordinate changes and Newton identities, which are part of Algebra and polynomial manipulation. However, those identities recover the hidden ideal structure, while the requested object is the canonical reduced Gröbner basis.
