# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

For each integer $n\geq1$, let $L_n$ be the splitting field over $\mathbb{Q}$ of
$$
g_n(x)=233x^{6\cdot2^n}-612x^{5\cdot2^n}-450x^{4\cdot2^n}+1356x^{3\cdot2^n}+592x^{2\cdot2^n}-549x^{2^n}-233.
$$
Determine $[L_n:\mathbb{Q}]$ exactly for every $n\geq1$.

## Domain Explanation

This problem involves splitting fields, cyclotomic subfields, Kummer extensions, and field degrees, which are part of Abstract Algebra and Field theory. The problem also involves prime-ideal valuations and power-residue obstructions, which are part of Number Theory and Modular arithmetic and congruences. However, those arithmetic tools detect relations among radical classes, while the requested quantity is controlled by the resulting field-extension structure.
