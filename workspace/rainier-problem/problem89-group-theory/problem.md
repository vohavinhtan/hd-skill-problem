# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $r\geq3$, let $E=\mathbb F_{2^r}$, and let
$$
V=\operatorname{End}_{\mathbb F_2}(E).
$$
For $a\in E$, write $M_a(x)=ax$, let $F(x)=x^2$, and let $\operatorname{Tr}:E\to\mathbb F_2$ be the absolute trace. For $X\in V$, let $X^\dagger$ be the adjoint with respect to $(x,y)\mapsto\operatorname{Tr}(xy)$.

Let $G$ be the group of all affine $\mathbb F_2$-maps $g:V\to V$ satisfying
$$
\operatorname{rank}(g(X)-g(Y))=\operatorname{rank}(X-Y)
\qquad(X,Y\in V).
$$
Define
$$
\mathcal C=\{M_a+M_bF:a,b\in E\},
\qquad
J(X)=X^\dagger F.
$$
For $C\in\mathcal C$, let $\tau_C(X)=X+C$, and put
$$
H=\langle\,\tau_C\ (C\in\mathcal C),\,J\,\rangle\leq G.
$$
Determine $|N_G(H)|$ as a closed formula in $r$.

## Domain Explanation

The problem asks for the normalizer of the affine symmetry group generated by translations of a natural two-term rank-metric code and its adjoint-Frobenius symmetry. The decisive structure is group-theoretic: rank-preservers, code idealizers, cyclic normalizers, and quotient fixed spaces.
