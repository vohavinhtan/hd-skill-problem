# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

For $n\geq 1$, let $\mathcal A_n$ be the set of words
$$
w=(w_0,w_1,\ldots,w_{n-1})\in\{0,1,2\}^n
$$
with indices read modulo $n$, satisfying all three conditions:
$$
w_i\ne w_{i+1}\quad\text{for every }i,
$$
$$
\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}w_i\equiv 0\pmod 3,
$$
and
$$
\#\{i:w_i=0\}\equiv 0\pmod 2.
$$

Let the twisted dihedral group action on such words be generated by
$$
\rho(w)_i=w_{i-1}
$$
and
$$
\tau(w)_i\equiv -w_{-i}\pmod 3.
$$
Let $a_n$ be the number of orbits of $\mathcal A_n$ under the group generated by $\rho$ and $\tau$.

Determine an exact formula for the sequence $(a_n)_{n\geq 1}$.

## Domain Explanation

This problem is primarily about counting constrained colorings up to a twisted dihedral symmetry. The strongest competing viewpoint is finite group actions because Burnside-type fixed sets appear, but the main task is enumerative: deriving the exact orbit-counting sequence under simultaneous adjacency, congruence, and parity constraints.
