# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $n\geq 19$ be odd, let $R>0$, and let $s$ be an integer such that
$$
2\leq s\leq \frac{n-3}{8},
\qquad
\gcd(s,n)=1.
$$
For $j\in\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z$, set
$$
U_j=
\left(
\cos\frac{2\pi j}{n},
\sin\frac{2\pi j}{n}
\right),
\qquad
V_j=
\left(
-\sin\frac{2\pi j}{n},
\cos\frac{2\pi j}{n}
\right).
$$
Choose real numbers $t_0,\ldots,t_{n-1}$ and define
$$
X_j=U_j+t_jV_j.
$$
Assume that
$$
\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}t_j=0,
\qquad
\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}X_j=(0,0),
\qquad
\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}t_j^2=R^2.
$$
For $q\in\{s,2s,4s\}$, define the algebraic area functional
$$
\mathcal A_q
=
\frac12\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}\det(X_j,X_{j+q}),
$$
where subscripts are read modulo $n$.

Determine exactly the attainable region
$$
\mathscr R_{n,s,R}
=
\left\{
(\mathcal A_s,\mathcal A_{2s},\mathcal A_{4s})\in\mathbb R^3:
\text{all the displayed constraints hold}
\right\}.
$$
Your answer must give an explicit list of all vertices and all facets of this region and must characterize every choice of $(t_j)_{j=0}^{n-1}$ that maps to its boundary.

## Domain Explanation

The requested object is a three-dimensional attainable convex region together with its complete vertex, facet, and boundary-fiber structure, so the primary classification is Topology and Geometry / Convex geometry. Euclidean geometry is the strongest competing classification because the region is generated by tangent displacements and algebraic areas of regular star circuits, but those constructions feed the convex-geometric face-classification problem rather than serving as the final target.
