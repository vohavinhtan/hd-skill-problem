# Problem

On the parabola $y=x^2$, choose two points

$$
P_a=(a,a^2)
$$

and

$$
P_b=(b,b^2),
$$

where the tangent lines to the parabola at $P_a$ and $P_b$ are perpendicular. Let $M=(X,Y)$ be the midpoint of the segment $P_aP_b$.

Determine the exact equation of the locus of all possible points $M$.

**Domain:** Topology and Geometry

**Sub-domain:** Coordinate geometry

**Problem Type:** Locus problem

**Answer Type:** Equation

**Difficulty:** Hard

**Domain Explanation:** This problem asks for the locus of midpoints determined by a geometric condition on tangents to a parabola. The solution translates perpendicularity of tangent lines into an algebraic relation between two parameters. The setting is coordinate geometry because the geometric condition and the final locus are both expressed through explicit equations.
