# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $p\geq5$ be a prime, let
$$
S=\mathbb F_p[x,y]
$$
with the standard grading, and set
$$
I_p=\left(x^{p+2},y^{p+2},(x+y)^{p+1},(x+2y)^{p+1}\right).
$$
Write
$$
A_p=S/I_p,
\qquad
\mathfrak m=(x,y)A_p,
$$
and let
$$
\operatorname{Soc}(A_p)=\{a\in A_p:\mathfrak m a=0\}.
$$

Determine the graded socle polynomial
$$
\Sigma_p(t)=\sum_{d\geq0}\dim_{\mathbb F_p}\!\left(\operatorname{Soc}(A_p)_d\right)t^d
$$
for every prime $p\geq5$.

## Domain Explanation

This problem studies the socle and homogeneous ideal structure of an Artinian quotient ring in positive characteristic. The decisive work is to determine how the defining ring relations change across degrees and then identify the annihilator of the maximal homogeneous ideal, so ring-theoretic ideal and annihilator structure is the primary mathematical content.
