# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $p$ be an odd prime, and let $L$ be the Lie algebra over $\mathbb F_p$ of strictly upper triangular $4\times4$ matrices, with bracket $[X,Y]=XY-YX$. Write $e_{ij}$ for the standard matrix units.

Let $\theta\in\operatorname{Aut}(L)$ be the involution defined by
$$
\theta(e_{12})=e_{34},\qquad
\theta(e_{23})=e_{23},\qquad
\theta(e_{34})=e_{12},
$$
$$
\theta(e_{13})=-e_{24},\qquad
\theta(e_{24})=-e_{13},\qquad
\theta(e_{14})=e_{14}.
$$
Determine
$$
\left|C_{\operatorname{Aut}(L)}(\theta)\right|
$$
as a closed formula in $p$.

## Domain Explanation

The problem asks for the centralizer of a diagram involution in the automorphism group of a finite-dimensional nilpotent Lie algebra. The decisive structure is algebraic: characteristic central-series quotients, induced bilinear and trilinear forms, automorphism lifting, and fixed-point counting.
