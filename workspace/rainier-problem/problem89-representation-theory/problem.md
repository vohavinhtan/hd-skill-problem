# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $p$ be an odd prime with $p\ne5$, and let
$$
V=\left\{(a_0,a_1,a_2,a_3,a_4)\in\mathbb F_p^5:\sum_{i=0}^4a_i=0\right\}.
$$
Let $L$ be the free nilpotent Lie algebra of class $3$ generated by $V$, i.e. the free Lie algebra on $V$ modulo all Lie monomials of bracket length at least $4$.

Define linear maps on $V$ by
$$
\rho(a_0,a_1,a_2,a_3,a_4)=(a_4,a_0,a_1,a_2,a_3),
$$
$$
\sigma(a_0,a_1,a_2,a_3,a_4)=(a_0,a_4,a_3,a_2,a_1).
$$
They extend uniquely to automorphisms of $L$. Put
$$
D=\langle\rho,\sigma\rangle.
$$
Determine
$$
\left|C_{\operatorname{Aut}(L)}(D)\right|
$$
as a closed formula in $p$.

## Domain Explanation

The problem asks for the centralizer of a dihedral action inside the automorphism group of a free nilpotent Lie algebra. The decisive structure is representation-theoretic: the augmentation module of a dihedral group, its endomorphism algebra, induced actions on free Lie powers, and equivariant IA-automorphisms.
