# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Fix an integer $r\geq21$ divisible by $21$. Let
$$
\widetilde S=\operatorname{SL}_2(\mathbb F_{27}),
\qquad
z=-I\in Z(\widetilde S),
\qquad
S=\widetilde S/\langle z\rangle=\operatorname{PSL}_2(\mathbb F_{27}).
$$
Put
$$
h(x)=(x^2+x+1)(x^6+x^5+x^4+x^3+x^2+x+1)\in\mathbb F_2[x].
$$
Since $21\mid r$, the polynomial $h(x)$ divides $x^r-1$ in $\mathbb F_2[x]$.

Identify $Z(\widetilde S^r)$ with $\mathbb F_2[x]/(x^r-1)$ by
$$
(\varepsilon_0,\ldots,\varepsilon_{r-1})
\longleftrightarrow
(z^{\varepsilon_0},\ldots,z^{\varepsilon_{r-1}})
\longleftrightarrow
\sum_{i=0}^{r-1}\varepsilon_i x^i.
$$
Let $D_r\leq Z(\widetilde S^r)$ correspond to the ideal generated by $h(x)$, and set
$$
N_r=\widetilde S^r/D_r.
$$
The cyclic shift of the $r$ factors preserves $D_r$; let $\tau$ denote the induced automorphism of $N_r$, and put
$$
G_r=N_r\rtimes\langle\tau\rangle,
\qquad |\tau|=r.
$$
Let
$$
\pi:G_r\to\langle\tau\rangle
$$
be the natural quotient map. For $0\leq i<r$, let
$$
\lambda_i:N_r\to S
$$
be the homomorphism induced by projection to the $i$-th factor of $\widetilde S^r$ followed by quotienting by $\langle z\rangle$.

Let $L_r/F_r$ be a finite Galois extension equipped with a fixed isomorphism
$$
\operatorname{Gal}(L_r/F_r)\cong G_r.
$$
Via this isomorphism, regard $N_r$ and $\ker\lambda_i$ as subgroups of $\operatorname{Gal}(L_r/F_r)$, and define
$$
C_r=L_r^{N_r},
\qquad
P_i=L_r^{\ker\lambda_i}.
$$

Call an intermediate field $E$, with $F_r\subseteq E\subseteq L_r$, admissible if

1. $E\cap C_r=F_r$;
2. $(EC_r)\cap P_i=C_r$ for every $0\leq i<r$;
3. the normal closure of $E/F_r$ is $L_r$.

Let
$$
\mu_r
=
\min\{[E:F_r]:E\text{ is admissible}\},
$$
and let $c_r$ be the number of $F_r$-isomorphism classes of admissible fields $E$ satisfying
$$
[E:F_r]=\mu_r.
$$

Determine the ordered pair
$$
(\mu_r,c_r)
$$
for every positive multiple $r$ of $21$.

## Domain Explanation

The problem asks for intermediate fields of a finite Galois extension subject to intersection, compositum, and normal-closure constraints. Through the Galois correspondence these field conditions become structural restrictions on subgroups of the explicitly specified Galois group, including a central lifting obstruction and cyclic conjugacy constraints. The requested minimum degree and isomorphism-class count are therefore invariants of the intermediate-field structure of a Galois extension.
