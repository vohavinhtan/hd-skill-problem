# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $\mathcal M$ be the set of all monic polynomials $P\in\mathbb R[x]$ of degree $6$. For $P\in\mathcal M$, define
$$
\Lambda(P)=\max_{-1\leq x\leq 1}\frac{|P(x)|}{\sqrt{1+120x^2}}.
$$
Determine exactly
$$
\Lambda_*=\min_{P\in\mathcal M}\Lambda(P).
$$

## Domain Explanation

This problem involves weighted minimax approximation by monic polynomials,
which are part of Optimization and Numerical Mathematics and Approximation theory.
The problem also involves polynomial interpolation and factorization,
which are part of Algebra, Functions, and Trigonometry and Polynomial and rational functions.
However, those algebraic tools certify the extremal value, while the main task is weighted approximation.
