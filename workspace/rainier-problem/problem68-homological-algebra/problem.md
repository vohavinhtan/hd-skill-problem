# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Fix integers
$$
a,b,c\geq3.
$$
Let $\Bbbk$ be a field. In the polynomial ring
$$
\Bbbk[x_1,\ldots,x_a,u_1,\ldots,u_b,v_1,\ldots,v_c],
$$
put
$$
X=\prod_{i=1}^{a}x_i,
\qquad
U=\prod_{j=1}^{b}u_j,
\qquad
V=\prod_{k=1}^{c}v_k.
$$
Let $I$ be generated by
$$
x_i^2,\qquad u_j^2,\qquad v_k^2
$$
for all admissible indices, by all cross-products
$$
x_i u_j,\qquad u_j v_k,\qquad v_k x_i,
$$
and by
$$
X-U,\qquad U-V.
$$
Define
$$
R=
\frac{\Bbbk[x_1,\ldots,x_a,u_1,\ldots,u_b,v_1,\ldots,v_c]}{I}.
$$
Put
$$
X_0=\prod_{i=1}^{a-1}x_i,
\qquad
U_0=\prod_{j=1}^{b-1}u_j,
\qquad
V_0=\prod_{k=1}^{c-1}v_k,
$$
and let
$$
z=X_0+U_0+V_0,
\qquad
M=R/(z).
$$
Let
$$
\mathfrak m=(x_1,\ldots,x_a,u_1,\ldots,u_b,v_1,\ldots,v_c),
$$
so that $R/\mathfrak m\cong\Bbbk$. For $n\geq0$, set
$$
\beta_n=\dim_{\Bbbk}\operatorname{Tor}_n^R(M,\Bbbk).
$$
Determine, as a rational function of $t$, the Poincaré series
$$
P_M^R(t)=\sum_{n\geq0}\beta_n t^n.
$$

## Domain Explanation

This problem is classified under Abstract Algebra and Homological Algebra because the requested object is the generating function of the Betti numbers in a minimal free resolution. Its derivation requires separating a three-branch fiber product from a two-dimensional socle identification, recognizing the cyclic module generated by a cross-branch near-socle element, constructing the corresponding relative resolution, and then transferring it through the socle quotient without losing the module-specific correction.
