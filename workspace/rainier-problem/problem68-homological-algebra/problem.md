# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Fix integers
$$
a,b,c\geq2.
$$
Let $\Bbbk$ be a field. In the polynomial ring
$$
\Bbbk[x_1,\ldots,x_a,u_1,\ldots,u_b,v_1,\ldots,v_c],
$$
put
$$
X=\prod_{i=1}^{a}x_i,
\qquad
U=\prod_{j=1}^{b}u_j,
\qquad
V=\prod_{k=1}^{c}v_k.
$$
Let $I$ be generated by
$$
x_i^2,\qquad u_j^2,\qquad v_k^2
$$
for all admissible indices, by all cross-products
$$
x_i u_j,\qquad u_j v_k,\qquad v_k x_i,
$$
and by
$$
X-U,\qquad U-V.
$$
Define
$$
R=
\frac{\Bbbk[x_1,\ldots,x_a,u_1,\ldots,u_b,v_1,\ldots,v_c]}{I}.
$$
Put
$$
X_0=\prod_{i=1}^{a-1}x_i,
\qquad
U_0=\prod_{j=1}^{b-1}u_j,
\qquad
V_0=\prod_{k=1}^{c-1}v_k,
$$
and let
$$
z=X_0+U_0+V_0,
\qquad
M=R/(z).
$$
Let
$$
\mathfrak m=(x_1,\ldots,x_a,u_1,\ldots,u_b,v_1,\ldots,v_c),
$$
so that $R/\mathfrak m\cong\Bbbk$. For $n\geq0$, set
$$
\beta_n=\dim_{\Bbbk}\operatorname{Tor}_n^R(M,\Bbbk).
$$
Determine, as a rational function of $t$, the Poincaré series
$$
P_M^R(t)=\sum_{n\geq0}\beta_n t^n.
$$

## Domain Explanation

This problem is classified under Abstract Algebra and Homological Algebra because the requested invariant is a Poincaré series defined from the $\operatorname{Tor}$ groups of a module, so minimal free resolutions and extension classes control the computation. A plausible secondary domain is Discrete Mathematics and Combinatorics because alternating words index bases in the relevant resolutions, but that counting is auxiliary rather than the mathematical object being classified. The ring is an Artinian connected sum obtained by identifying three socle generators, and the module is defined by a sum of three branchwise near-socle monomials. The derivation must track the two-dimensional socle quotient, the resulting resolution correction, and the extension class of the cyclic ideal generated by $z$. The requested output is therefore homological and is naturally expressed as one rational generating function.
