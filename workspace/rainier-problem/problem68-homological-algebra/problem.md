# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Fix integers
$$
a,b,c,s\geq2.
$$
Let $\Bbbk$ be a field. Define the commutative $\Bbbk$-algebra
$$
R=\frac{\Bbbk[x_1,\ldots,x_a,u_1,\ldots,u_b,v_1,\ldots,v_c,y_1,\ldots,y_s]}{I},
$$
where $I$ is generated by
$$
x_i^2,\quad u_j^2,\quad v_k^2,\quad y_\ell^2,
$$
for all admissible indices, together with
$$
y_\ell x_i,\quad y_\ell u_j,\quad y_\ell v_k
$$
for every admissible pair of indices. Put
$$
X=\prod_{i=1}^{a}x_i,
\qquad
U=\prod_{j=1}^{b}u_j,
\qquad
V=\prod_{k=1}^{c}v_k,
$$
and define the $R$-module
$$
M=R/(y_1,\ldots,y_s,XU,UV,VX).
$$
Let
$$
\mathfrak m=(x_1,\ldots,x_a,u_1,\ldots,u_b,v_1,\ldots,v_c,y_1,\ldots,y_s),
$$
so that $R/\mathfrak m\cong\Bbbk$. For $n\geq0$, set
$$
\beta_n=\dim_{\Bbbk}\operatorname{Tor}_n^R(M,\Bbbk).
$$
Determine, as a rational function of $t$, the Poincaré series
$$
P_M^R(t)=\sum_{n\geq0}\beta_n t^n.
$$

## Domain Explanation

This problem is classified under Abstract Algebra and Homological Algebra because its requested object is the generating function of the Betti numbers in a minimal free resolution. The derivation requires reconstructing a minimal resolution of a three-generator monomial module with a common socle intersection, then transferring that resolution across the decomposition of the ambient local algebra into two branches with zero cross-products.
