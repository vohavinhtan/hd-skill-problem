# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $p\geq5$ be prime, let $e\geq1$, and put
$$
q=p^e.
$$
Let
$$
\mathcal Q_q=\{az^2+bz+c:a,b,c\in\mathbb F_q\}.
$$
Define a simple graph $\Gamma_q$ on the vertex set $\mathcal Q_q$ by joining two distinct polynomials $f$ and $g$ precisely when
$$
f(x)=g(x)
$$
for at least one $x\in\mathbb F_q$.

Let $V_q=\mathbb C^{\mathcal Q_q}$ with its standard Hermitian inner product, and let $A_q:V_q\to V_q$ be the adjacency operator of $\Gamma_q$:
$$
(A_qF)(f)=\sum_{g\sim f}F(g).
$$
Define the permutation $\tau:\mathcal Q_q\to\mathcal Q_q$ and its induced unitary operator $U_q:V_q\to V_q$ by
$$
(\tau f)(z)=f(z+1),
\qquad
(U_qF)(f)=F(\tau^{-1}f).
$$

For $u,v\in\mathbb F_q$, set
$$
\mathcal P_{u,v}=\{f\in\mathcal Q_q:f(u)=v\},
$$
and let $W_q\subseteq V_q$ be the span of the indicator functions
$$
\mathbf 1_{\mathcal P_{u,v}}
\qquad(u,v\in\mathbb F_q).
$$
Let $\Pi_q$ be the orthogonal projection of $V_q$ onto $W_q^\perp$, and define
$$
T_q=\left.\Pi_qU_qA_q\right|_{W_q^\perp}.
$$

Determine, as a polynomial in $X$, the characteristic polynomial
$$
\chi_{p,e}(X)=\det\left(XI-T_q\right)
$$
in closed form as a function of $p$ and $q$.

## Domain Explanation

The primary object is the spectrum of a finite graph built from intersections of quadratic polynomial graphs over a finite field. The requested characteristic polynomial is not the ordinary adjacency spectrum: the adjacency operator is first coupled to a nontrivial order-$p$ graph automorphism and then compressed away from the incidence space generated by all common-point pencils. Recovering the result requires identifying the graph's translation structure, resolving the quadratic root obstruction, determining the exact Fourier support of the pencil space, and counting the automorphism orbits inside the remaining eigenspaces.
