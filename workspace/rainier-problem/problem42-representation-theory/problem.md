# Normalized Math Problem

## LaTeX (Normalized)

Let $n\geq5$, let $p>30n$ be prime, and let $V$ be an $n$-dimensional vector space over $\mathbb F_p$. Let $U\in\operatorname{GL}(V)$ be regular unipotent, so $U-I$ is one nilpotent Jordan block of size $n$.

Put $\lambda=(4,3,2,1)$ and let $W=\mathbb{S}_\lambda(V)\subseteq V^{\otimes10}$, realized as the image of a row-then-column Young symmetrizer of shape $\lambda$. Let $A$ be the action of $U^{\otimes10}$ on $W$, and set $N=A-I_W$.

On $W^{\otimes3}$, let $S_3$ act by place permutations, and define
$$
z=(1+(12))(1-(13)),\qquad
Z=z(W^{\otimes3}),\qquad
C=\left.N^{\otimes3}\right|_Z.
$$
On $Z^{\otimes3}$, let $C_i$ apply $C$ in the $i$-th factor and the identity in the other two. The following symmetric combinations preserve $\Lambda^3Z$:
$$
E_1=C_1+C_2+C_3,\qquad
E_2=C_1C_2+C_1C_3+C_2C_3,\qquad
E_3=C_1C_2C_3.
$$
Let $I=I_{\Lambda^3Z}$ and define
$$
B=\det
\begin{pmatrix}
E_1&I&0\\
2E_2&E_1&I\\
3E_3&E_2&E_1
\end{pmatrix}
\in\operatorname{End}_{\mathbb F_p}(\Lambda^3Z),
$$
where the determinant is expanded by the Leibniz formula in the commuting algebra generated by $E_1,E_2,E_3$.

For $r\in\{0,1,2\}$, let $\delta_r(n)=1$ when $n\equiv r\pmod 3$, and let $\delta_r(n)=0$ otherwise. Determine the ordered pair $(L_n,\mu_n)$, where $L_n$ is the largest nilpotent Jordan-block size of $B$ and $\mu_n$ is its multiplicity. The complete Jordan form is not requested.

## Domain Explanation

This problem involves Young symmetrizers, Schur modules, and regular unipotent actions, which are part of Abstract Algebra and Representation theory. The problem also involves nilpotent Jordan blocks, tensor operators, and exterior powers, which are part of Linear Algebra. However, linear algebra records the final operator behavior, while the main reductions come from symmetric group representations and principal weight strings, so Abstract Algebra is the more appropriate primary classification.
